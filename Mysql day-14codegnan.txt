DATE&TIME  FUNCTIONS
-----------------------------------------
-CURDATE():   Current date.
CURTIME():   Current time.
NOW():   Current date and time.
DATE():  Extract date part.
TIME():   Extract time part.
YEAR():   Extract year.
MONTH():  Extract month.
DAY()/DAYOFMONTH(): Extract day of the month.
WEEK():   Extract week number.
DAYOFWEEK():  Extract day of the week.
DATE_ADD():  Add time to a date.
DATE_SUB():  Subtract time from a date.
DATEDIFF(): Difference between two dates.
TIMESTAMPDIFF():  Difference between two DATETIME values.
STR_TO_DATE(): Convert string to date.
DATE_FORMAT():  Format a date.
FROM_UNIXTIME():  Convert Unix timestamp to date.
UNIX_TIMESTAMP():  Convert date to Unix timestamp.
